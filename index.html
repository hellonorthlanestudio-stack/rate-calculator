Questions creators ask

What is CPM for creators?

CPM means cost per mille, the price per 1,000 views. If your videos average 20,000 views and you charge a $25 CPM, your base rate is 20 × $25 = $500. You choose your own CPM based on your niche, engagement and past results.

Should I charge extra for usage rights?

Usage rights let a brand reuse your content, for example as a paid ad or on their own channels, for a set period. That gives the brand more value than a single post, so many creators add a percentage on top, often more for longer periods.

What is exclusivity in a brand deal?

Exclusivity means you agree not to work with the brand's competitors for a period of time. It limits the other deals you can take, so creators usually charge more for it.

How do I price UGC videos that I don't post?

For UGC, views on your own account don't apply, so creators usually charge a flat fee per video and add usage rights on top when the brand runs the video as an ad.

Is this the "right" rate?

There's no single right rate. This calculator turns your own numbers into a consistent quote so you're not guessing in the DMs. Raise your CPM or base fee as your views, engagement and results grow.
